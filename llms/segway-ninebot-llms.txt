# Segway-Ninebot

> Global personal-transportation and robotics company (Ninebot acquired Segway in 2015). Designs and manufactures electric kickscooters — the world's top-selling e-kickscooter brand, 13M+ units across 100+ countries — self-balancing transporters, e-bikes, and, through Segway Robotics, RMP mobile-robot chassis and delivery/service robots (Nova Carter, NVIDIA Isaac developer kits). No first-party REST/OpenAPI product API is currently published; the public developer surface is open-source ROS / NVIDIA Isaac integration packages.

## Company
- [Segway website](https://segway.com): Consumer products — electric kickscooters, e-bikes, personal transporters.
- [Segway Store](https://store.segway.com): Product storefront.
- [Segway Robotics](https://robotics.segway.com/): Robotics division — RMP chassis, Nova Carter, delivery robots.
